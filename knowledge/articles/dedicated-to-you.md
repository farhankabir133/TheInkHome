Photo by author via AI Can you believe it has already been a month? On May 23rd, I hit publish for the very first time on Medium. I remember sitting at my laptop, wondering if my words would just float away into an empty void. But today, on June 23rd, I look at my notifications and my feed, and I realize something beautiful: I didn’t just find a platform to post my articles. I found a family. Whether you’ve been reading my pieces since day one or you just stumbled across my profile five minutes ago, this milestone belongs to you. For me, this first month hasn’t just been about sharing my own writing; it has been about discovering yours. Medium is a massive, bustling library, and I have fallen completely in love with being a reader here. Every single day, I love scrolling through my feed, diving into your thoughts, highlighting the sentences that make me stop and think, and chatting with you all in the comments. Every green notification dot that lights up on my screen feels like a genuine conversation over coffee with a friend. I am so incredibly grateful for how welcoming, kind, and supportive everyone has been. You all make this feel like home. A few voices that keep me inspired There is an absolute ocean of talent on this platform, and I am genuinely a fan of everyone who takes the time to share their thoughts here. It’s impossible to list every single person who inspires me, but there are a few specific writers whose work has consistently stood out to me and kept me coming back for more this month. I want to take a quick moment to shine a spotlight on them: Thank you Otba . There is something special about you, that I am only subbed to you, because your writing pieces deserve places in my inbox. Thank you Pari Sharma . You always drop the most comforting gems of wisdom in the comments. As a young writer, having your support on here makes me feel like we’re all surviving and growing together. Thank you A Fresh Mindset . Reading your work feels like a breath of fresh air. Your perspective is always so pure and authentic, and I’m just a massive fan of your mind. 💙 Thank you, Swati . You write things that nobody else is saying, and I respect that so much. Every time you publish a new piece, I know I’m in for something brilliant. 🤩 Thank you, Shalveena Rohde . The first thing that hooked me was, when you wrote about journaling. And everyone knows that diaries are my soulmates. 💖 First month celebration!! The Gen Z Circle (A.K.A. The Absolute Coolest Energy) ⚡ Thanks a million, ~usmyy . Honestly, finding another Gen Z writer on here who just gets it has been the best plot twist of my first month.
