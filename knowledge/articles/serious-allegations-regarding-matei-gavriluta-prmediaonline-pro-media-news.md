After trying for nearly three years to resolve this matter privately, I am now forced to speak publicly about a serious payment dispute involving Matei Gavriluta ( PRMediaOnline / Pro Media News). In 2022, based on a long professional relationship in the digital marketing and PR industry, I agreed to help process a client payment on his request. The payment of approximately $34,000 USD was received through an account arranged only to facilitate the transaction. After payment processing charges, exchange deductions, and conversion costs, approximately $25,016 USD was transferred in cryptocurrency to the wallet address provided by Matei Gavriluta. Later, the original client initiated a payment dispute. Because the transaction had been processed through the receiving account, that account was left with a significant negative balance and has remained unusable for almost three years. Since then, I have made repeated attempts to resolve the issue privately. Over time there were multiple conversations where the matter was expected to be settled or repaid, yet the financial liability remains unresolved. As a result of the ongoing situation, the owner of the account that was used to receive the payment has now filed a complaint against me based on the banking transaction trail. My involvement in this matter was limited to assisting with payment processing based on trust built over years of professional collaboration. The funds were transferred to the wallet address that was provided. Despite this, the financial and legal consequences of the dispute have been left unresolved. Because of this, I have now begun formal complaints and legal actions through the appropriate authorities in order to protect myself and recover from the damage caused by this situation. I am sharing this publicly so that professionals in the digital marketing, PR, and link-building industry understand the risks involved when handling cross-border payments or intermediary transactions on behalf of others.
